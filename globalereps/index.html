Energy-Saving Motors

Adventech Motor Generators

Adventech motor generators reduce energy consumption by 15 - 30 percent and improve the plant's power factor overall by eliminating excess KVAR. These motor generators can update existing motors that need to be rewound, or provide new power saving motor generators for new machines or replacements for existing installations.

Adventech
  • Produces higher torque than conventional motors
  • Eliminates the Reactive energy (KVAR) at any load
  • Reduces amps of nearby motors and improves power factor for free
  • Reduces amps between 15% - 30% at full load
  • Significantly reduce energy and operation costs
  • Reduces amps between 50% - 90% at no load
  • Reduction in KW/H consumption between 3% to 10%
  • Unlimited Stops and Starts per hour
  • Accepts shaft overloads up to 200%
